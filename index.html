About Me

I am a doctoral student at KTH Royal Institute of Technology. My research focuses on numerical modelling and analysis of fluid transport and engineering processes in fractured rock, with particular emphasis on cement grout propagation, fracture-network flow, geothermal energy extraction, and coupled multiphysics processes.
